Summer in China can be scorching, but with the right planning, you can enjoy a fantastic trip without breaking the bank or melting in the heat. From snagging cheap flights and car rental deals to staying cool with innovative gadgets, this guide covers everything you need for a comfortable and affordable summer adventure.

## Table of contents

## Best Time to Visit for Deals

Summer (June to August) is peak travel season in China, but also a time when many airlines and rental companies offer promotions to attract travellers. The best deals often appear around the "618" shopping festival (mid-June) and last through July. If you're flexible, consider travelling in early June or late August to avoid the highest crowds and temperatures while still enjoying summer activities.

## Getting Around: Flights and Car Rentals

![Baomo Garden, Guangdong, Guangzhou](/img/destinations/summer-travel-china-tips/1.webp)

### Flight Deals

Several Chinese travel platforms like Fliggy (飞猪, Fēi Zhū) offer summer flight cards starting as low as ¥329 for routes to popular destinations in Fujian and Guangdong provinces, including Xiamen, Fuzhou, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen. These cards typically require booking within a set period (e.g., until early July) and are valid for travel through the summer. Keep an eye out for "blind box" flight deals that let you save ¥173 on any route.

### Car Rental Discounts

Renting a car is a great way to explore China's coastal and rural areas at your own pace. Major platforms like eHi (一嗨, Yī Hāi) offer summer promotions:

- **5-day rental card**: from ¥699, valid until August 2026 (excludes holidays)
- **New user special**: 2 days for ¥198
- **Weekly commuter card**: 2 days for ¥239 (Monday–Thursday only)
- **Daily rental from ¥110** in 32 cities including Chongqing, Sanya, Xishuangbanna, Lijiang, and Qingdao

Many deals include free cancellation if not used, and some offer discounts up to ¥2000 for electric vehicles when you share the promotion with friends.

## Top Summer Destinations in China

![Kejia Earthen Tulou, Fujian](/img/destinations/summer-travel-china-tips/2.webp)

### Fujian Province
- **Pingtan Island (平潭岛, Píngtán Dǎo)**: Famous for its "Blue Tears" – bioluminescent plankton that glow at night. Best viewed in summer.
- **Xiamen (厦门, Xià Mén)**: A coastal city with colonial architecture, Gulangyu Island, and fresh seafood.
- **Fuzhou (福州, Fú Zhōu)**: Known for hot springs, ancient alleys, and the Three Lanes and Seven Alleys historic area.

### Guangdong Province
- **Guangzhou (广州, Guǎng Zhōu)**: A food lover's paradise with dim sum, Cantonese barbecue, and night markets.
- **Shenzhen (深圳, Shēn Zhèn)**: A modern metropolis with theme parks, shopping, and beach escapes.
- **Nan'ao Island (南澳岛, Nán'ào Dǎo)**: A quiet island with pristine beaches, seafood, and stunning sunsets.

### Other Cool Escapes
- **Qingdao (青岛, Qīng Dǎo)**: A beach city with German colonial architecture and the famous Tsingtao Beer.
- **Lijiang (丽江, Lì Jiāng)**: A UNESCO World Heritage old town in Yunnan, with cooler mountain temperatures.
- **Xishuangbanna (西双版纳, Xīshuāngbǎnnà)**: Tropical rainforests, Dai culture, and elephant sanctuaries.

## Practical Tips for Summer Travel

### Beat the Heat
- **Stay hydrated**: Carry a reusable water bottle; many attractions have free water refill stations.
- **Plan outdoor activities early morning or late afternoon**: Avoid midday sun (11:00–15:00).
- **Use portable fans and cooling towels**: Widely available at convenience stores and online.
- **Wear light, breathable clothing**: Linen and cotton are your friends.

### Save Money
- **Book flights and rental cars during promotional periods**: June's "618" sales offer significant discounts.
- **Use public transport**: China's high-speed rail network is efficient and affordable. For example, a second-class ticket from Guangzhou to Shenzhen costs about ¥100.
- **Eat like a local**: Street food and small eateries are cheap and delicious. Avoid tourist-heavy restaurants near major attractions.

### Mobile Payment and Connectivity
- **Alipay (支付宝, Zhīfùbǎo) and WeChat Pay (微信支付, Wēixìn Zhīfù)**: Essential for most transactions. Link your international credit card or use a prepaid travel card.
- **VPN**: If you need access to Google, Facebook, or Instagram, install a reliable VPN before arriving.
- **SIM card**: Buy a local SIM at the airport or use an eSIM from providers like Holafly.

### Visa and Entry
- **144-hour transit visa**: Available at major cities like Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Chengdu for citizens of 53 countries. Check eligibility before travel.
- **Tourist visa (L visa)**: Most travellers need a visa. Apply at least a month in advance.

## Where to Stay

For summer travel, consider accommodations with air conditioning and swimming pools. Budget options include youth hostels (¥50–150 per night) and express hotels like Hanting or Home Inn (¥150–300). Mid-range hotels (¥300–600) often offer better amenities. For a unique experience, try a traditional courtyard hotel (四合院, sìhéyuàn) in Beijing or a water town guesthouse in Suzhou.

## Sample 5-Day Summer Itinerary: Fujian Coast

**Day 1: Arrive in Xiamen**
- Explore Gulangyu Island (ferry ¥35 return).
- Evening: Zhongshan Road night market for seafood.

**Day 2: Xiamen to Pingtan**
- Drive or take a high-speed train (about 3 hours).
- Visit Pingtan's stone villages and beaches.
- Night: Watch for Blue Tears at Tannan Bay.

**Day 3: Pingtan to Fuzhou**
- Morning: Explore Haitan Ancient City.
- Afternoon: Drive to Fuzhou (2 hours).
- Evening: Visit Three Lanes and Seven Alleys.

**Day 4: Fuzhou to Quanzhou**
- Morning: Drive to Quanzhou (1.5 hours).
- Visit Kaiyuan Temple and the Maritime Museum.
- Afternoon: Enjoy local snacks like oyster omelette.

**Day 5: Return to Xiamen**
- Stop at Meizhou Island for Mazu culture.
- Depart from Xiamen.

## FAQ

**Q: Is summer too hot to travel in China?**
A: While it can be hot, especially in the south, many destinations have cooling sea breezes or mountain climates. Plan indoor activities during midday and use portable fans.

**Q: How do I book flights and rental cars as a foreigner?**
A: Platforms like Fliggy (飞猪) and eHi (一嗨) have English interfaces or can be used with translation tools. You'll need a Chinese phone number or Alipay account for booking.

**Q: Are portable fans allowed on flights?**
A: Yes, but lithium-ion battery-powered fans must be in carry-on luggage. Check airline rules for battery capacity limits.

**Q: What should I do if I get heatstroke?**
A: Move to a shaded or air-conditioned area, drink cool water, and apply a cold compress. Seek medical help if symptoms persist.

**Q: Can I use my foreign driver's license in China?**
A: Foreign driver's licenses are not valid. You need an International Driving Permit (IDP) or a Chinese temporary driving permit. Some rental companies may accept a notarized translation.

**Q: Are there any festivals in summer?**
A: The Dragon Boat Festival (端午节, Duānwǔ Jié) in June features dragon boat races and zongzi (sticky rice dumplings). The Qixi Festival (七夕节, Qīxī Jié) in August is China's Valentine's Day.
