Summer in eastern China can be scorching, but Jiangsu (江苏) and Zhejiang (浙江) provinces offer a cool retreat with their lush mountains, crystal-clear streams, and innovative water parks. From river tracing (溯溪, sùxī) in emerald pools to splashing in futuristic water worlds, this guide covers the top water activities and the best new hotels to base your adventure. Whether you're a family with kids or a couple seeking romance, these destinations promise a refreshing escape.

## Table of contents

## Top Water Activities in Jiangsu and Zhejiang

![Jiangnan Water Town Scenery, Suzhou](/img/destinations/jiangsu-zhejiang-summer-water-activities/1.webp)

### River Tracing (溯溪) in Xianju, Taizhou
Xianju County (仙居县) in Taizhou (台州), Zhejiang, is renowned for its "glass water" — streams so clear they look like liquid jade. The area around Danzhu Township (淡竹乡) is a hotspot for river tracing, where you wade through shallow, rocky streams, sometimes climbing over small waterfalls. The water is cool even in peak summer, with average temperatures around 24°C. It's a perfect activity for families and nature lovers.

### Water Parks and Splash Zones
For a more structured water experience, Zhejiang boasts several large water parks. The **Hangzhou Parallel World Water Park** (杭州平行世界水乐园) is a new sci-fi themed indoor-outdoor park spanning over 30,000 sqm with nearly 20 attractions. Another favorite is the **Qiandao Lake Happy Water World** (千岛湖欢乐水世界), offering kayaking, water trampolines, and obstacle courses. In Jiaxing, the **Kaiyuan Senbo Water Park** (嘉兴开元森泊水乐园) is the largest in Zhejiang among the Senbo chain, covering 31,000 sqm.

### Lakeside and Seaside Swimming
Qiandao Lake (千岛湖, Qiān Dǎo Hú) is a man-made reservoir with hundreds of islands, offering swimming and water sports at resorts like the **Qiandao Lake InterContinental** (now renamed Freedom InterContinental Hall). For a seaside vibe, head to **Zhoushan** (舟山) islands, where new hotels like the **Swissotel Zhoushan** provide pools and easy access to beaches.

## Best New Hotels for Water Activities

### Taizhou Xianju Huajianfu · Ziyuan Hotel (台州神仙居花间府·紫垣酒店)
This newly opened Huazhu-brand hotel is located in the heart of Danzhu Township, next to the Shenxianju (神仙居) scenic area. It offers 107 rooms with mountain or garden views. The hotel features a children's playground, a meditation space, and direct access to the stream for river tracing. Packages start from ¥998 for two nights, including breakfast and a choice of a river tracing picnic basket or dinner. Summer weekends and Mid-Autumn Festival are included at no extra charge.

### Qiandao Lake Freedom InterContinental Hall (千岛湖自由洲际殿)
Formerly the InterContinental Qiandao Lake, this resort sits on the Xianshan Peninsula. It boasts a 1,800 sqm kids' club, indoor and outdoor pools, and a private beach. The summer package at ¥1,699 for two nights includes a double pass to the Qiandao Lake Happy Water World (13 activities), breakfast, and mini-bar. Weekdays in summer are free of surcharge.

### Jiaxing Kaiyuan Senbo Resort (嘉兴开元森泊度假酒店)
Opened in 2024, this resort has the largest water park among Zhejiang's Senbo properties. The package at ¥699 per night includes breakfast and two tickets to either the water park or the indoor fantasy island. It's ideal for families, with a music fountain show every evening.

### Tonglu Shilili Mange · Daqishan Villa Resort (桐庐诗莉莉漫戈塔·大奇山Villa度假酒店)
Located at the foot of Daqishan Mountain in Tonglu County, this resort blends Jiangnan garden aesthetics with modern comfort. It offers indoor and outdoor children's play areas, and packages start from ¥899 for two nights with afternoon tea. The hotel is close to Daqishan National Forest Park and the Fuchun River.

## Getting There and Around

### By High-Speed Rail
- **To Taizhou Xianju**: From Shanghai, take a train to Taizhou Station (about 2.5 hours), then a 30-minute drive to the hotel. From Hangzhou, it's 1.5 hours by train.
- **To Qiandao Lake**: High-speed trains run from Hangzhou to Qiandao Lake Station (about 1 hour). The resort offers shuttle bus from the station.
- **To Tonglu**: Trains from Hangzhou take 30 minutes. The hotel is a short taxi ride from Tonglu Station.

### By Car
Driving is convenient for exploring multiple spots. Major highways connect all destinations. Navigation apps like **Gaode Maps** (高德地图) or **Baidu Maps** (百度地图) work well with Chinese place names.

### Local Transport
Taxis and ride-hailing (via **Alipay** or **WeChat**) are widely available. For river tracing spots, renting a car or hiring a driver is recommended as public transport is limited.

## Food Recommendations

### Local Delicacies
- **Xianju**: Try **yangmei cake** (杨梅糕), a sweet treat made from local bayberries, and **tumian noodles** (土麦面), a rustic wheat noodle soup. For dinner, order **yangmei spareribs** (杨梅子排) and **mountain chicken clay pot** (山地土鸡煲).
- **Qiandao Lake**: Freshwater fish dishes like **sturgeon** and **fish head soup** are must-tries.
- **Tonglu**: The area is known for **Fuchun River fish** and bamboo shoot dishes.

### Dining at Hotels
Most hotels offer on-site restaurants featuring local cuisine. The Huajianfu hotel's restaurant serves Xianju specialties with a view of the garden. The Qiandao Lake InterContinental has multiple dining options including a buffet and Chinese restaurant.

## Best Time to Visit
Summer (June to September) is the prime season for water activities. July and August are the hottest but also the most vibrant. Early June and September offer milder weather and fewer crowds. Weekdays are generally less busy.

## Costs and Budgeting

![Shi Lake Scenery, Jiangsu, Suzhou](/img/destinations/jiangsu-zhejiang-summer-water-activities/3.webp)

- **Accommodation**: Packages range from ¥699 to ¥1,699 per night, often including breakfast and activity tickets.
- **Food**: Local meals cost about ¥50-100 per person. Hotel restaurants are pricier but convenient.
- **Transport**: High-speed rail from Shanghai to Taizhou is around ¥200 one way. Car rental with driver costs about ¥500-800 per day.
- **Tickets**: Water park tickets are typically ¥100-200 per person. River tracing is free if you go independently.

## Practical Tips for International Travelers

### Payment
**Alipay** (支付宝) and **WeChat Pay** (微信支付) are widely accepted. You can link your international credit card to these apps. Carry some cash for small vendors.

### Visa
Most travelers need a visa to enter China. However, the **144-hour visa-free transit** policy applies in Shanghai, Hangzhou, and Nanjing for citizens of 53 countries. Ensure you meet the conditions (e.g., arriving from and departing to a third country).

### Language
English is not widely spoken in rural areas. Download a translation app like **Google Translate** or **Pleco**. Learn basic phrases like "Thank you" (谢谢, xièxiè) and "How much?" (多少钱, duōshǎo qián).

### What to Pack
- **Water shoes** for river tracing (rocks can be slippery).
- **Sunscreen** and a hat.
- **Insect repellent** for evenings.
- **Swimwear** and a quick-dry towel.
- **Power bank** for your phone.

### Etiquette
- Remove shoes when entering temples or some traditional accommodations.
- Respect nature: don't litter, and avoid disturbing wildlife.
- Quiet hours in hotels are typically after 10 PM.

## Suggested Itineraries

- **Day 1**: Arrive at Taizhou, transfer to Huajianfu hotel. Afternoon river tracing in Danzhu stream. Evening dinner at hotel.
- **Day 2**: Morning visit to Shenxianju scenic area (cable car, cliff walks). Afternoon free time for swimming or hiking. Try local snacks.
- **Day 3**: Explore Shawan Ancient Village (20 min drive) for more clear water and koi fish. Depart.

### 4-Day Family Fun (Qiandao Lake + Tonglu)
- **Day 1**: Arrive at Qiandao Lake, check into Freedom InterContinental. Afternoon at the water park.
- **Day 2**: Morning boat tour of the lake. Afternoon at the resort's kids' club or feeding swans.
- **Day 3**: Drive to Tonglu (about 2 hours). Check into Shilili Mange. Explore Daqishan National Forest Park.
- **Day 4**: Visit Yaolin Fairyland (瑶琳仙境) caves or Fuchun River. Depart.

## FAQ

### What is river tracing and do I need experience?
River tracing involves walking upstream in a shallow river, often over rocks and small waterfalls. No special skills are required, but you should be comfortable in water and wear sturdy water shoes. Children can join easy sections.

### Are these hotels foreigner-friendly?
Yes, all listed hotels accept international guests. Staff may have limited English, but booking platforms like Ctrip (Trip.com) have English interfaces. Hotels can assist with taxi bookings and basic inquiries.

### Can I use my credit card?
International credit cards (Visa, Mastercard) are accepted at major hotels and some restaurants, but for smaller shops and taxis, Alipay or cash is preferred. Set up Alipay before your trip.

### Is it safe to swim in the streams?
Yes, the streams in Xianju are clean and generally safe. However, avoid swimming after heavy rain when currents may be stronger. Always supervise children.

### What is the 144-hour visa-free transit?
It allows citizens of 53 countries to stay in designated areas (e.g., Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu) for up to 144 hours without a visa, provided you have a confirmed onward ticket to a third country. Check eligibility before travel.

### Are there English signs at water parks?
Major water parks have some English signage, but it's limited. Download a translation app and familiarize yourself with key terms. Staff at ticket counters may understand basic English.
