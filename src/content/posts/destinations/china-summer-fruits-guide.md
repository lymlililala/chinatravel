Summer in China is a fruit lover's paradise. From the fragrant, juicy peaches of Sichuan to the burst-in-your-mouth bayberries of Zhejiang, the season offers an abundance of fresh, flavorful produce that is deeply woven into local culture and cuisine. This guide takes you on a tour of China's top summer fruits, where they grow, how to eat them, and tips for incorporating fruit adventures into your travels.

## Table of contents

## Top Summer Fruits in China

### Sichuan Peaches (四川水蜜桃)
Sichuan's Longquanyi (龙泉驿) district, known as the "Hometown of Peaches in China," produces some of the country's finest peaches. The **Baifeng (白凤)** variety is a standout: thin-skinned, tender, and bursting with sweet juice. Grown in the unique microclimate of the Longquan Mountains, these peaches benefit from ample sunshine, large temperature swings, and mineral-rich purple soil. They are harvested from late June to July, with a sugar content often exceeding 17°.

![Ripe Sichuan peach held in hand, showing juicy flesh and thin skin](https://images.pexels.com/photos/20030797/pexels-photo-20030797.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

### Zhejiang Bayberries (浙江杨梅)
Zhejiang's **Xianju (仙居)** county is famous for its **Dongkui (东魁)** bayberries, which can be as large as ping-pong balls. These deep red, knobby berries are prized for their perfect balance of sweet and tart, with an intense fruity aroma. Grown on high-altitude slopes with spring water irrigation, they are hand-picked at midnight and shipped via cold-chain express to ensure freshness. The season is short – only about 15 days in late June to early July.

![Freshly picked Xianju bayberries, large and dark red, with leaves](https://images.pexels.com/photos/12177805/pexels-photo-12177805.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

### Shaanxi Nectarines (陕西夏梦水蜜桃)
Shaanxi's **Xiameng (夏梦)** peach is a late-season gem that fills the gap between Sichuan and Jiangsu harvests. Grown in the fertile Guanzhong Plain, these peaches are tree-ripened for at least 8 days of sun exposure, resulting in a rich, old-fashioned peach flavor with a hint of acidity. They are remarkably affordable – often under ¥40 for a 2.5-pound box – and arrive firm but ripen quickly to a soft, juicy state.

### Hubei Blood Peaches (湖北血桃)
From **Zaoyang (枣阳)**, Hubei – another "Hometown of Peaches" – comes the **Blood Peach (血桃)**, a variety with deep red flesh rich in anthocyanins. When firm, it's crisp and slightly tart; when fully ripe, it becomes soft, sweet, and aromatic. These peaches have a history dating back to the Tang Dynasty and were once tribute fruit for emperors.

## Where to Buy Summer Fruits in China

![Tianfuguosheyingyi, Sichuan, Chengdu](/img/destinations/china-summer-fruits-guide/1.webp)

### Local Markets
Visiting a local fruit market is the best way to experience seasonal produce. Look for:
- **Farmers' markets (菜市场)** in small cities and towns
- **Fruit stalls (水果摊)** on street corners – often the freshest and cheapest
- **Supermarkets** like Hema (盒马) or Freshhema for reliable quality

### Online Shopping
Many Chinese consumers order fruits directly from growers via WeChat mini-programs or e-commerce platforms like **Taobao** and **Pinduoduo**. For travelers, this is less practical unless you have a Chinese address and payment method.

### Direct from Orchards
If you're visiting fruit-producing regions, many orchards offer **pick-your-own (采摘)** experiences. This is especially popular around Chengdu (for peaches) and Xianju (for bayberries). Check with local tourism offices or ask your hotel concierge.

## How to Enjoy Summer Fruits

### Fresh and Simple
Most summer fruits are best eaten raw, at room temperature or slightly chilled. Peaches and nectarines can be washed and eaten out of hand; bayberries should be rinsed gently in salted water for 10 minutes to remove any insects or debris.

### In Drinks and Desserts
- **Peach iced tea** or **peach smoothies**
- **Bayberry soda** – drop a few berries into sparkling water
- **Frozen bayberries** – a natural sorbet-like treat
- **Peach sorbet** – blend ripe peaches with a little sugar and freeze

### In Local Dishes
- **Peach glutinous rice balls (桃子汤圆)** – a sweet soup from Jiangnan
- **Bayberry wine (杨梅酒)** – a traditional summer drink made by steeping berries in baijiu or vodka

## Getting There & Around

### Sichuan (Longquanyi)
- **By train**: Take a high-speed train to **Chengdu East Station** (成都东站), then a local bus or taxi to Longquanyi (about 30 minutes).
- **By metro**: Chengdu Metro Line 2 reaches Longquanyi station.

### Zhejiang (Xianju)
- **By train**: High-speed trains from **Hangzhou** to **Xianju South Station** (仙居南站) take about 1.5 hours. From there, take a local bus or taxi to bayberry orchards (20-30 minutes).
- **By car**: About 2.5 hours from Hangzhou via G15 expressway.

### Shaanxi (Guanzhong Plain)
- **By train**: Take a high-speed train to **Xi'an** (西安), then rent a car or join a tour to visit peach orchards in the surrounding countryside.

### Hubei (Zaoyang)
- **By train**: High-speed trains from **Wuhan** to **Zaoyang** (枣阳) take about 1 hour. Local taxis can take you to peach farms.

## Practical Tips for International Travelers

### Payment
Most fruit vendors accept **Alipay** and **WeChat Pay**. Cash is also accepted at traditional markets. Credit cards are rarely used.

### Language
Learn a few key phrases:
- "How much?" – 多少钱? (Duōshao qián?)
- "I want to buy this fruit" – 我想买这个水果 (Wǒ xiǎng mǎi zhège shuǐguǒ)
- "Is it sweet?" – 甜吗? (Tián ma?)

### Etiquette
- **Don't touch fruit unnecessarily** – use the provided tongs or ask the vendor to pick.
- **Negotiate politely** at markets, but not at supermarkets or fixed-price stalls.
- **Tipping is not expected**.

### Storage
- Peaches: Store at room temperature to ripen, then refrigerate for up to 3 days.
- Bayberries: Refrigerate immediately and eat within 2 days; freeze for longer storage.
- Nectarines: Same as peaches.

## Suggested Itinerary: A Fruit-Focused Trip

![Lugu Lake Ligeban Island Scenery, Sichuan](/img/destinations/china-summer-fruits-guide/3.webp)

**Day 1-2: Chengdu & Longquanyi**
- Visit Longquanyi peach orchards (June-July).
- Enjoy a peach-themed lunch at a local farmhouse.
- Explore Chengdu's wide and narrow alleys (宽窄巷子) for fruit desserts.

**Day 3-4: Xianju, Zhejiang**
- Take morning high-speed train from Chengdu to Hangzhou, then to Xianju.
- Visit bayberry orchards for pick-your-own (late June-early July).
- Try bayberry wine and bayberry ice cream in town.

**Day 5-6: Xi'an & Guanzhong**
- Fly or take high-speed train from Hangzhou to Xi'an.
- Visit peach orchards in the Guanzhong Plain (July-August).
- Explore Xi'an's Muslim Quarter for fresh fruit juices and dried fruits.
