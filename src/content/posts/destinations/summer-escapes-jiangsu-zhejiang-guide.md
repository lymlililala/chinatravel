Escape the summer heat in China's lush Jiangsu and Zhejiang provinces, where misty mountains, serene lakes, and bamboo forests offer a refreshing retreat. This guide curates the best summer getaways—from the iconic Moganshan mountain resort to the tranquil Tonglu countryside—with insider tips on boutique hotels, nature activities, and practical travel advice for international visitors. Whether you're seeking a family-friendly resort, a romantic hideaway, or an adventure in the great outdoors, these destinations promise cool breezes and unforgettable experiences.

## Table of contents

## Top Summer Destinations in Jiangsu and Zhejiang

Both provinces are dotted with scenic escapes that are especially popular during the hot months (June–September). Here are the standout spots:

### Moganshan (莫干山, Mògàn Shān)

A legendary summer resort since the early 20th century, Moganshan is known for its bamboo-covered hills, cool climate, and historic villas. It's located in Deqing County, about a 2-hour drive from Shanghai or Hangzhou. The area offers hiking trails, waterfalls, and boutique hotels that blend into the landscape. Many international travelers come here for a weekend of fresh air and tranquility.

![Bamboo forest path in Moganshan with a historic stone villa](https://images.pexels.com/photos/24033231/pexels-photo-24033231.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

### Tonglu (桐庐, Tónglú)

Tonglu, part of Hangzhou municipality, is famous for its karst landscapes, the Fuchun River, and ancient villages. It's a paradise for hikers and photographers. The area has seen a surge in quality resorts, making it a perfect base for nature exploration. Summer temperatures are several degrees cooler than in the city.

### Anji (安吉, Ānjí)

Known as the "Bamboo Sea," Anji is a lush county in Zhejiang with vast bamboo forests, tea plantations, and the scenic Tianhuangping area. It's a popular spot for eco-tourism and family trips, with many resorts offering outdoor activities like zip-lining and mountain biking.

### Suzhou (苏州, Sūzhōu)

While Suzhou is famous for its classical gardens and canals, the outskirts offer summer escapes like Taihu Lake and Suzhou乐园 (Suzhou Amusement Land). The Suzhou Longemont Hotel mentioned in deals is near the amusement park and wetlands, ideal for families.

![Suzhou classical garden with a lotus pond in summer](https://images.pexels.com/photos/34675319/pexels-photo-34675319.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

## Best Time to Visit

The ideal time for summer escapes is **June to September**, with July and August being peak season. Expect temperatures around 25–30°C (77–86°F) in the mountains, cooler than the cities. Rain is common, especially in June (plum rain season), so pack an umbrella. Weekdays are less crowded, and many hotel deals offer "weekend surcharges" but remain affordable.

## Getting There & Around

### By Air
- **Moganshan**: Fly to Hangzhou Xiaoshan International Airport (HGH) or Shanghai Pudong (PVG), then take a 2-hour taxi or high-speed train to Deqing Station.
- **Tonglu**: Fly to Hangzhou, then take a 1-hour taxi or bus to Tonglu.
- **Anji**: Fly to Hangzhou, then 1.5-hour drive. Alternatively, take a high-speed train to Anji Station.

### By High-Speed Rail
China's extensive rail network connects major cities to these destinations:
- **Deqing Station** (for Moganshan): 13 minutes from Hangzhou, 1 hour from Shanghai.
- **Tonglu Station**: 30 minutes from Hangzhou.
- **Anji Station**: 30 minutes from Hangzhou.

### Getting Around
- **Taxis and Ride-Hailing**: Use Didi (China's Uber) with Alipay or WeChat Pay. Have your destination written in Chinese.
- **Car Rental**: International brands like Hertz are available, but driving requires an International Driving Permit (IDP) or a Chinese license. Many hotels offer shuttle services.
- **Buses**: Local buses serve scenic spots, but schedules may be infrequent. Taxis are more convenient for foreigners.

## Suggested Itineraries

### 3-Day Moganshan Nature Escape
- **Day 1**: Arrive at Deqing Station, check into Radisson Vika Hotel. Afternoon hike to Moganshan's Sword Pond (剑池). Evening: dinner at a local farmhouse.
- **Day 2**: Explore the Moganshan National Tourist Resort: visit the historic villas, bamboo forest, and waterfalls. Lunch at a mountain café. Afternoon: visit Xiazhuhu Lake.
- **Day 3**: Morning walk in the hotel's garden, then depart.

### 4-Day Tonglu & Anji Adventure
- **Day 1**: Arrive in Tonglu, check into Xizi Yunshan Jiuli Resort. Afternoon: explore Daqi Mountain National Forest Park.
- **Day 2**: Full-day rafting on the Fuchun River or visit the Yaolin Fairyland (瑶琳仙境) caves.
- **Day 3**: Drive to Anji (1 hour). Check into Lin Jian treehouse. Afternoon: visit the Bamboo Sea scenic area.
- **Day 4**: Morning at the treehouse, then depart.

## Food & Dining

Local cuisine in Jiangsu and Zhejiang is light, fresh, and seasonal. Must-try dishes:
- **Moganshan**: Bamboo shoot dishes, wild vegetables, and local chicken.
- **Tonglu**: Fuchun River fish, braised pork with preserved vegetables.
- **Anji**: Bamboo rice (bamboo tube filled with rice and meat), tea-flavored dishes.
- **Suzhou**: Songshu guiyu (squirrel-shaped mandarin fish), xiaolongbao (soup dumplings).

Many hotels offer meal packages. For authentic local food, visit village restaurants or food streets like Suzhou's Guanqian Street.

## Practical Tips for International Travelers

- **Visa**: Most visitors need a tourist visa (L visa). Check if you qualify for the 144-hour transit visa if you're passing through Shanghai, Hangzhou, or Nanjing.
- **Mobile Payment**: Alipay and WeChat Pay are widely accepted. Set them up before your trip with an international credit card. Carry some cash (¥200–500) for small vendors.
- **Language**: Download a translation app like Pleco or Google Translate. Major hotels have English-speaking staff.
- **Etiquette**: Remove shoes when entering some traditional accommodations. Tipping is not customary.
- **Packing**: Light clothing, rain jacket, comfortable walking shoes, insect repellent, and sunscreen.

## Costs & Budget

- **Accommodation**: Deals range from ¥249/night (budget) to ¥800/night (luxury). Peak season (July–August) prices are higher.
- **Meals**: Local restaurants: ¥50–100 per person. Hotel buffets: ¥150–300.
- **Transport**: High-speed rail from Shanghai to Deqing: ¥100–150. Taxi from Deqing to Moganshan: ¥80–100.
- **Activities**: Hiking trails are free; theme parks: ¥200–300 per person.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to speak Chinese to travel in Jiangsu and Zhejiang?**
A: Not necessarily. Major hotels and tourist attractions often have English signage and staff. However, learning a few phrases like "hello" (nǐ hǎo) and "thank you" (xiè xiè) helps. Download a translation app for convenience.

**Q: Are the hotel deals refundable?**
A: Yes, most promotional hotel deals are fully refundable if canceled before the check-in date. Always read the fine print. The deals mentioned here are from reputable platforms like Ctrip.

**Q: What is the best way to book these hotels?**
A: Use Chinese travel apps like Ctrip (Trip.com for English) or Fliggy. Many deals require a Chinese phone number for verification; you can ask your hotel to assist or use a friend's number. Alternatively, book through international sites like Booking.com, though deals may differ.

**Q: Is it safe to drink tap water?**
A: No, tap water is not potable. Drink bottled water, which is cheap and widely available. Hotels provide free bottled water.

**Q: Can I use my credit card everywhere?**
A: International credit cards (Visa, Mastercard) are accepted at major hotels and some restaurants, but many small shops and taxis prefer mobile payment or cash. Always carry some cash.

**Q: Are there any cultural taboos I should know?**
A: Avoid pointing with your finger; use an open hand. Do not stick chopsticks upright in a bowl of rice, as it resembles incense used at funerals. When entering a temple, dress modestly and avoid loud behavior.
