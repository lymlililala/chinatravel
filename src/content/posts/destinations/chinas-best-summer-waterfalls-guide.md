Summer in China brings monsoon rains that transform rivers into roaring cascades, making it the prime season for waterfall viewing. From the world's widest calcium tufa falls in Jiuzhaigou to the golden torrents of Hukou on the Yellow River, this guide covers the most spectacular waterfalls across the country. Whether you're a nature lover, photographer, or adventure seeker, these thundering waters offer unforgettable experiences. We'll provide practical tips on when to go, how to reach each site, and what else to see nearby.

## Top Waterfalls to Visit in Summer

![Pan Gate, Suzhou](/img/destinations/chinas-best-summer-waterfalls-guide/1.webp)

### 1. Huangguoshu Waterfall (黄果树瀑布, Huáng Guǒ Shù Pù Bù) – Guizhou Province

**Asia's largest waterfall** and China's most famous cascade, Huangguoshu is a must-see. It stands 77.8 m high and 101 m wide, with a powerful curtain of water that creates rainbows on sunny afternoons. The waterfall is part of a larger scenic area that includes **Doupotang** and **Tianxingqiao** sections, where you can walk behind the waterfall (Water Curtain Cave) for a unique perspective.

- **Best time:** June–August (peak flow; July–August can be very crowded).
- **Getting there:** Take a high-speed train to **Anshun West Station** (from Guiyang, ~30 min), then a bus or taxi (~40 km).
- **Tickets:** ¥160 (peak season); book online via WeChat or travel platforms.
- **Nearby:** Visit **Tianlong Tunpu** (Ming dynasty fortress village) or **Longgong Cave** (dragon palace karst cave).

### 2. Detian Waterfall (德天瀑布, Dé Tiān Pù Bù) – Guangxi Province

**Asia's largest transnational waterfall**, shared with Vietnam's Ban Gioc Waterfall. The combined width exceeds 250 m, and the water drops over three main tiers amidst lush karst peaks. You can take a bamboo raft to the base for a close-up view.

- **Best time:** June–September (water volume peaks; avoid weekends for fewer crowds).
- **Getting there:** From **Nanning**, take a bus to Daxin County (3 hours), then a local bus or taxi to the scenic area.
- **Tickets:** ¥115 (including sightseeing car).
- **Visa note:** You can see Vietnam from the river but cannot cross without a visa.
- **Nearby:** Explore **Mingshi Tianyuan** (rural karst landscape) or **Chongzuo** for dinosaur fossils.

### 3. Hukou Waterfall (壶口瀑布, Hú Kǒu Pù Bù) – Shaanxi/Shanxi Border

**The world's largest yellow (golden) waterfall** on the Yellow River. In summer, the river swells to over 1,000 m wide, creating a thundering, mud-colored cascade that seems to boil. The name "Hukou" means "kettle mouth" — the river narrows into a gorge like water pouring from a spout.

- **Best time:** June–August (also spectacular in late May and September).
- **Getting there:** Two viewing sides: **Shaanxi side** (Yichuan County) and **Shanxi side** (Jixian County). From **Xi'an**, take a bus to Yichuan (4 hours), then local transport. High-speed rail to **Yan'an** then bus is also possible.
- **Tickets:** ¥100 (Shaanxi side), ¥120 (Shanxi side).
- **Tip:** The Shaanxi side offers a more frontal view; the Shanxi side is closer to the main drop.
- **Nearby:** Combine with a visit to **Yan'an** (Red tourism) or **Pingyao Ancient Town**.

### 4. Nuorilang Waterfall (诺日朗瀑布, Nuò Rì Lǎng Pù Bù) – Sichuan Province

**China's widest waterfall** (270 m) and the largest **calcium tufa waterfall** in the country. Located in **Jiuzhaigou Valley**, a UNESCO World Heritage site, the water flows over a broad, moss-covered travertine ledge, surrounded by pristine forest. In summer, the turquoise pools upstream contrast with the white curtain of the falls.

- **Best time:** June–August (water volume high; autumn colors also popular but summer is lush).
- **Getting there:** Fly to **Jiuzhaigou Huanglong Airport** from Chengdu, then take a bus (1.5 hours). Alternatively, take a bus from Chengdu (8 hours).
- **Tickets:** Jiuzhaigou entrance ¥169 (peak season) + sightseeing bus ¥90.
- **Note:** Nuorilang is a stop on the park's bus route; allow a full day for the park.
- **Nearby:** **Huanglong Scenic Area** (colorful travertine pools) is 2 hours away.

### 5. Chishui Waterfall (赤水大瀑布, Chì Shuǐ Dà Pù Bù) – Guizhou Province

**China's largest danxia (red sandstone) waterfall**, 76 m high and 80 m wide. It lies in the **Chishui Danxia** UNESCO World Heritage area, where thousands of waterfalls cascade over red cliffs. The surrounding forest and bamboo groves add to the scenery.

- **Best time:** June–August.
- **Getting there:** High-speed train to **Chishui Station** from Guiyang (2 hours), then bus to the scenic area.
- **Tickets:** ¥80 (Chishui Grand Waterfall scenic area).
- **Nearby:** Explore **Sishan Miao Village** or **Bamboo Sea National Park**.

### 6. Malingshan Gorge Waterfall Cluster (马岭河峡谷瀑布群) – Guizhou Province

**China's densest canyon waterfall cluster** with over 100 waterfalls in the wet season, some dropping 200 m. The gorge is a deep fissure with lush vegetation and calcium carbonate deposits on the walls. A boardwalk along the canyon floor gives you a front-row seat.

- **Best time:** June–August (peak flow; May–September good).
- **Getting there:** **Xingyi City** is the gateway. Fly to **Xingyi Wanfenglin Airport** from major cities, or take a bus from Guiyang (4 hours).
- **Tickets:** ¥70 (gorge) + optional cable car.
- **Nearby:** **Wanfenglin** (thousands of karst peaks) is a short drive away.

### 7. Xiaolangdi Reservoir (小浪底水库) – Henan Province

While not a natural waterfall, Xiaolangdi's **annual water-sediment regulation** (June–July) creates a man-made spectacle: the dam releases vast amounts of water and sediment, turning the Yellow River into a churning golden torrent. The contrast between clear upstream water and muddy downstream flow is dramatic.

- **Best time:** Late June to early July (check exact dates annually).
- **Getting there:** From **Luoyang**, take a bus or taxi (1 hour).
- **Tickets:** ¥40 (scenic area).
- **Tip:** Visit early morning to see the release; the event usually lasts a few weeks.

## Suggested Itineraries

### 3-Day Guizhou Waterfall Tour
- **Day 1:** Arrive in Guiyang, visit **Huangguoshu** (half-day).
- **Day 2:** Travel to **Chishui** (high-speed train), explore **Chishui Grand Waterfall** and **Sishan Miao Village**.
- **Day 3:** Visit **Malingshan Gorge** in Xingyi, then depart.

### 5-Day Guangxi & Guizhou Waterfalls
- **Day 1:** Arrive in Nanning, transfer to Daxin.
- **Day 2:** **Detian Waterfall** + **Mingshi Tianyuan**.
- **Day 3:** Drive to Guizhou (4–5 hours), visit **Huangguoshu**.
- **Day 4:** Explore **Malingshan Gorge**.
- **Day 5:** Depart from Xingyi or Guiyang.

## Getting Around & Practical Tips

![Wangshi Garden Scenery, Suzhou](/img/destinations/chinas-best-summer-waterfalls-guide/2.webp)

- **High-speed rail:** Connects most major cities; book via **12306** app (English version available).
- **Mobile payment:** Alipay and WeChat Pay are widely accepted; link your international credit card.
- **Visa:** Most travelers need a tourist visa (L). The 144-hour transit visa is available in select cities (e.g., Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou) — check eligibility.
- **What to bring:** Raincoat/umbrella, waterproof shoes, insect repellent, and a camera with a waterproof case.
- **Etiquette:** Don't litter; respect barriers; avoid climbing on wet rocks.

## Where to Stay

![Huqiu Scenery Mingshengqu Scenery, Suzhou](/img/destinations/chinas-best-summer-waterfalls-guide/3.webp)

- **Near Huangguoshu:** Stay in **Anshun** city (hotels from ¥200) or at the scenic area (guesthouses from ¥150).
- **Near Detian:** **Daxin County** has mid-range hotels (¥150–300); consider a homestay in **Mingshi** for rural charm.
- **Near Hukou:** **Yichuan County** (Shaanxi) or **Jixian County** (Shanxi) have basic hotels (¥100–200).
- **Near Jiuzhaigou:** Stay in **Zhangzha Town** (¥200–500) or inside the park (luxury options only).

## Best Time to Visit

- **June–August:** Peak waterfall flow, but also peak tourist season and higher prices. Aim for weekdays.
- **May & September:** Shoulder months with decent flow and fewer crowds.
- **Avoid:** Winter (low water, some falls freeze) and Chinese public holidays (Golden Week in October, May Day).

## FAQ

**Q: Are the waterfalls safe to visit in summer?**  
A: Yes, but paths can be slippery. Stay on designated walkways, obey warning signs, and avoid climbing rocks near water. Some areas may close during heavy rain.

**Q: Can I use credit cards at these scenic spots?**  
A: Most ticket offices accept Alipay/WeChat Pay; credit cards are less common. Carry some cash (¥100–200) for small vendors.

**Q: Do I need to book tickets in advance?**  
A: For popular sites like Huangguoshu and Jiuzhaigou, yes — book online via official WeChat accounts or travel platforms (e.g., Ctrip) to avoid queues.

**Q: Is it possible to visit multiple waterfalls in one trip?**  
A: Yes, especially in Guizhou where Huangguoshu, Chishui, and Malingshan are within a few hours' drive. Plan 3–5 days for a comprehensive tour.

**Q: What is the 144-hour transit visa and can I use it?**  
A: It allows a 6-day stay in certain cities (e.g., Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou) without a full visa. You must arrive and depart from the same city and have a confirmed onward ticket. Not all waterfall destinations are covered; check eligibility.
