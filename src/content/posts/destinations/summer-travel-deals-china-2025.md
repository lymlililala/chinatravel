## Top Summer Deals at a Glance

### Airfare: Fuel Surcharge Drop

From July 5, 2025, China's domestic airfare fuel surcharge will decrease significantly: for flights under 800 km, the surcharge drops from ¥80 to ¥50; for flights over 800 km, it falls from ¥150 to ¥100. This means savings of ¥30–50 per ticket. If you're planning a trip, book after July 5 to lock in the lower rate.

To find the cheapest flights, use the "Special Offer" and "Price Alert" features on Fliggy. The Special Offer tool lets you search multiple origins and destinations for up to six months, sorted by price. Price Alert monitors fares 24/7 and notifies you when a target price is met.

### Hotel Loyalty Challenges

**Marriott Platinum Challenge (via Fliggy)**

From July 1 to August 31, 2025, 88VIP members who are Marriott members (below Platinum tier) can join a challenge: spend ¥2,000 on qualifying stays via Fliggy's Marriott flagship store to earn 120 days of Platinum status (room upgrades, late checkout, welcome gifts, 50% bonus points). If you complete 8 nights during the 120-day window, you keep Platinum status permanently. This is a fantastic way to unlock elite perks for a summer trip.

**Hilton Honors Flash Sale**

From July 1 to July 3, Hilton Home2 Suites (希尔顿惠庭) offers up to 8% off when booking 3 days in advance, plus bonus points that can yield over 50% value back. For example, at the Ningbo Hangzhou Bay New District property, you can earn up to 5,300+ points worth ¥180+. Other Hilton brands like Hampton by Hilton also offer 20x mileage redemption (up to 10% off room rates) from July 1 to July 4.

From July 1 to July 4, use Fliggy miles at 20x value to offset up to 10% of room rates at select Wanda properties in Quanzhou, Nanjing, Hangzhou, Dongguan, and Kunming. These cities offer rich culture and summer escapes.

## Visa Tips for Summer Travel

### 144-Hour Visa-Free Transit

China's 144-hour visa-free transit policy now covers 37 ports, including Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Chengdu, and Xi'an. Citizens of 54 countries can stay up to 6 days without a visa if they hold a confirmed onward ticket to a third country. This is perfect for a short summer layover. Always check the latest policy on the official Chinese immigration website before traveling.

### Visa-Free Destinations for Chinese Tourists

If you're a foreign resident in China, consider nearby visa-free destinations for a quick getaway. For example, Singapore offers visa-free entry for Chinese passport holders (and many others). Flights from major Chinese cities take just 4–6 hours. Singapore's summer deals include packages like Shanghai–Singapore round-trip flights from ¥1,699 and hotel stays at the Pan Pacific from ¥4,599 for two nights.

## How to Book and Pay

### Use Chinese Travel Platforms

For the best deals, use Fliggy (飞猪) or Ctrip (携程). Both have English versions and accept international credit cards, but Alipay or WeChat Pay (linked to a foreign card) are smoother. Download and set up Alipay before your trip – it's widely accepted.

### Understand the Deals

Most hotel deals are "book now, pay later" – you can cancel or get a full refund if unused. Always check the validity period and blackout dates. Summer deals often have no surcharge on weekends but may require a supplement for National Day (October 1–7).

## Food: Must-Try Summer Dishes

Summer in China means fresh, seasonal flavors. Don't miss:
- **Litchi (荔枝)** in Guangdong and Fujian – July is peak season.
- **Cold noodles (凉面)** in Sichuan and Shaanxi.
- **Grilled seafood** at coastal cities like Qingdao and Xiamen.
- **Mango pomelo sago (杨枝甘露)** – a classic Cantonese dessert.

## Best Time to Visit

Summer (June–August) is hot and humid in most of China, but it's also the season for festivals, outdoor activities, and discounted travel. The best strategy: head to cooler destinations like Yunnan (Kunming), Qinghai, or the northeast (Harbin, Dalian). Alternatively, embrace the heat with water parks and indoor attractions.

## Practical Tips for International Travelers

- **Mobile Payment**: Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay before arrival. Link a Visa/Mastercard. Many small vendors don't accept cash.
- **Transport**: High-speed trains connect major cities. Book tickets via Ctrip or 12306 (English version available). For metro, buy a rechargeable transport card.
- **Language**: Download a translation app like Pleco or Google Translate. Key phrases: "多少钱" (how much), "谢谢" (thank you).
- **Etiquette**: Remove shoes when entering homes, avoid pointing with chopsticks, and respect temple customs.
