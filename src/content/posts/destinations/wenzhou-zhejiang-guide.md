Wenzhou (温州) has a global reputation as China's most entrepreneurial city — the origin of the "Wenzhou model" of private economy that influenced China's economic reforms. Every Italian leather shoe district, every French pocket watch market, every Brazilian shoe factory has a Wenzhou community. But the city itself, surrounded by dramatic coastal mountains and the ancient Nanxi River valley, is a surprisingly rewarding destination.

## Table of contents

## Yandang Mountains (雁荡山)

One of China's most dramatically scenic mountain ranges — sharp volcanic rock peaks, waterfalls, and sea cliffs concentrated in a relatively small area north of Wenzhou. Often called "the most beautiful mountains south of the Yangtze."

**Lingfeng Scenic Area (灵峰景区):** The central and most dramatic section. The **Lingfeng Temple (灵峰寺)** is framed by two soaring rock towers; in the evening light the formations take different animal shapes depending on the viewing angle.

**Dalong Waterfall (大龙湫):** The most impressive waterfall in Zhejiang — a 197-metre single-drop waterfall visible from a valley floor approach. Best in late spring and after rain when the flow is highest. Ticket ¥60.

**Lingyan Scenic Area (灵岩景区):** A different landscape — broad river valley with walls of stratified volcanic rock, accessed by a combination of hiking paths and cableways.

## Nanxi River Valley (楠溪江)

100 km north of Wenzhou, the Nanxi River winds through a granite gorge valley containing some of the best-preserved traditional village architecture in Zhejiang.

**Furong Village (芙蓉村):** A Tang-Dynasty-founded village with a unique "seven stars and eight buckets" layout — the streets arranged according to astronomical symbolism. The ancestral hall, clan houses, and village walls are extraordinary examples of Song and Ming vernacular architecture.

**Yanxia Old Village (岩头镇):** A Song Dynasty planned village with intact defensive walls, 800-year-old water management systems, and a main street of traditional shophouses that has functioned continuously for a millennium.

**Bambooo rafting (竹筏漂流):** The traditional way to travel the Nanxi — rented bamboo rafts through clear water between sandstone and granite cliffs, with glimpses of kingfishers and white egrets.

## Practical Tips

**Getting there:** Wenzhou Longwan Airport (WNZ) — well-connected; high-speed rail from Shanghai (2.5 hrs), Hangzhou (2 hrs), Fuzhou (1.5 hrs).

**Combine with:** The Yandang Mountains + Nanxi River Valley + Wenzhou city can be covered in 3–4 days. The high-speed rail connection to Fuzhou makes Wenzhou a natural stop on a Zhejiang–Fujian coastal journey.
