Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region (宁夏回族自治区) is one of China's smallest and least-visited autonomous regions — a narrow corridor along the Yellow River where, for 200 years (1038–1227 AD), a people called the Tangut ruled an empire called Western Xia (西夏) that controlled the Silk Road's most lucrative section. Their tombs are still here. So is the Yellow River, and the desert it barely holds at bay.

## Western Xia Imperial Tombs (西夏王陵)

25 km west of Yinchuan (银川) — the imperial tombs of the Western Xia Kingdom, an empire founded by the Tangut people who are now one of China's smallest ethnic groups (or effectively extinct as a distinct community). The kingdom was obliterated by the Mongols in 1227; their writing system was deciphered only in the 1980s.

**The tombs:** Nine imperial tombs spread across the desert plain below the Helan Mountains — circular earthen mounds that look like eroded Mayan pyramids, some 25 metres tall. Unlike the elaborate stone architecture of Ming or Qing imperial tombs, Western Xia tombs are primarily earthen — but the setting, with the desert floor and the mountain backdrop, creates an atmosphere of isolated grandeur.

**The mystery:** The Western Xia Kingdom was deliberately erased from history by the Mongols — no reliable Chinese historical records survive. The tomb occupants, their rituals, and much of their culture remain partially unclear. The museum on site explains what archaeologists have reconstructed.

**Ticket:** ¥75. Open daily 8:00 AM – 5:30 PM.

## Shapotou (沙坡头)

Where the Yellow River curves through the Tengger Desert edge at Zhongwei (中卫) — one of the most extraordinary geographic juxtapositions in China: the river running bright and clear between sculpted sand dunes on one side and the irrigated Ningxia valley on the other.

**Desert activities:**
- **Sandboarding (沙坡滑沙):** The main Shapotou slope is a natural sand dune dropping to the river — sand sledges are available; the view from the top of the dune over the river and opposite shore is the defining Shapotou image
- **Zip-line over the Yellow River (过黄河):** Crossing the Yellow River by zip-line — genuinely exciting at the right water levels
- **Sheepskin raft (羊皮筏子):** Traditional rafts made from inflated whole sheepskins — the traditional Ningxia Yellow River crossing method, now offered as tourist rides

**Ticket:** ¥120 (includes access to main dune areas).

## Helan Mountains (贺兰山)

The mountain range that separates Ningxia from Inner Mongolia — archaeologically extraordinary. The **Helan Mountain Rock Paintings (贺兰山岩画)** contain over 6,000 individual petroglyphs from multiple historical periods (Paleolithic through historical) — the largest concentration of accessible ancient rock art in northwest China.

Subject matter: human figures, animals (wild sheep, deer, tigers, horses), celestial symbols, hunting scenes. The oldest examples are estimated at 10,000+ years.

**Ticket:** ¥75. Getting there: 50 km from Yinchuan by car.

## Hui Muslim Cuisine (宁夏清真菜)

Ningxia is China's heartland of Hui Muslim culture — the Hui are ethnically Han Chinese by appearance but practice Sunni Islam (and have done so since Tang Dynasty merchants established trading communities along the Silk Road).

**Lamb noodles (手抓羊肉):** Ningxia lamb raised on the arid steppe grass around the Yellow River bend is considered among the finest in China — lower fat than northern breeds, with a cleaner flavour. Hand-grabbed whole pieces served with salt and cumin.

**Yellow River carp (黄河鲤鱼):** The Yellow River carp of Ningxia — raised in the clear cold water of the upper Yellow River — is celebrated for its texture. Prepared simply, with ginger and scallion.

**Ningxia wine:** The Helan Mountains foothills have produced internationally award-winning wines since the 1980s — the semi-arid climate and diurnal temperature variation create conditions comparable to Burgundy or Napa. Several estate wineries offer tours and tastings (Helan Qingxue Vineyard, Silver Heights).

## Practical Tips

**Getting to Yinchuan:** Yinchuan Hedong Airport (INC) — flights from major cities. High-speed rail from Lanzhou (3.5 hrs), Xi'an (5 hrs).

**Itinerary:** Yinchuan (Western Xia Tombs, Helan Mountain rock art) → Zhongwei (Shapotou, 2 hrs by bus) makes a tight 3-day circuit.
