Summer in China can be scorching, but the right road trip leads to cool escapes: desert sunsets, turquoise lakes, coastal breezes, and mountain retreats. Whether you rent a car or hire a driver, these curated routes offer stunning landscapes and cultural gems. Buckle up for an unforgettable journey.

## Table of contents

## Top Summer Road Trip Routes

### 1. Dunhuang: Desert Gold & Ancient Caves (大漠金棕)

**Route:** Dunhuang city → Mingsha Mountain (鸣沙山) → Mogao Caves (莫高窟)
**Best time:** Late afternoon (enter after 4 PM to avoid scorching sand)

![Camel caravan crossing sand dunes at sunset, Dunhuang](https://images.pexels.com/photos/33412587/pexels-photo-33412587.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

Dunhuang (敦煌, Dūnhuáng) in Gansu Province is a gateway to the Silk Road. The **Mingsha Mountain** (鸣沙山, Míngshā Shān) offers camel rides and sandboarding. Climb the dunes for a panoramic view of the Crescent Moon Spring (月牙泉, Yuèyá Quán). The desert turns a rich golden-brown at sunset—a photographer's dream.

**Must-do:** Visit the **Mogao Caves** (莫高窟, Mògāo Kū), a UNESCO World Heritage site with stunning Buddhist murals. Book tickets online in advance (limited daily entry).

**Practical tips:**
- **Getting there:** Fly to Dunhuang Airport (DNH) from major cities like Beijing or Xi'an. Rent a car or hire a driver for the desert routes.
- **Accommodation:** Stay in Dunhuang city (e.g., Silk Road Dunhuang Hotel).
- **Cost:** Mogao Caves tickets around ¥200-300; camel ride at Mingsha Mountain about ¥100.
- **Mobile payment:** Alipay and WeChat Pay widely accepted; carry some cash for small vendors.

### 2. Sanya: Coastal Blues & Island Escapes (沧海银蓝)

**Route:** Sanya → Tianya Haijiao (天涯海角) → Wuzhizhou Island (蜈支洲岛)
**Best time:** Year-round, but summer offers fewer crowds and lower prices.

![Turquoise water and white sand beach at Wuzhizhou Island, Sanya](https://images.pexels.com/photos/12736037/pexels-photo-12736037.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

Sanya (三亚, Sānyà) on Hainan Island is China's tropical paradise. Drive along the coast to **Tianya Haijiao** (天涯海角, "Edge of the Sky, Rim of the Sea") for romantic sunset views. **Wuzhizhou Island** (蜈支洲岛, Wúzhīzhōu Dǎo) boasts crystal-clear waters perfect for snorkeling and diving.

**Must-do:** Take a boat to Wuzhizhou Island (ferry from Houhai Village, ¥140 round trip). Snorkel among coral reefs.

**Practical tips:**
- **Getting there:** Fly to Sanya Phoenix International Airport (SYX). Rent a car or use Didi (Chinese Uber).
- **Accommodation:** Beach resorts in Yalong Bay or Dadonghai.
- **Cost:** Island ferry ¥140; snorkeling gear rental ¥50-100.
- **Etiquette:** Remove shoes before entering some temples; dress modestly.

### 3. Sayram Lake: Alpine Meadow & Turquoise Water (赛里木青)

**Route:** Yining → Sayram Lake (赛里木湖) → Guozigou Bridge (果子沟大桥)
**Best time:** June–August for wildflowers and mild weather.

![Crystal clear lake surrounded by green meadows and snow-capped mountains, Sayram Lake](https://images.pexels.com/photos/37469103/pexels-photo-37469103.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

Sayram Lake (赛里木湖, Sàilǐmù Hú) in Xinjiang is known as "the last tear of the Atlantic" due to its deep blue color. The 90-km ring road (speed limit 50 km/h) offers endless photo ops. Hike up **Songshutou** (松树头) for a panoramic view.

**Must-do:** Visit **Kele Yongzhu** (克勒涌珠) for morning swans; **West Sea Grassland** (西海草原) for summer flower blooms.

**Practical tips:**
- **Getting there:** Fly to Yining Airport (YIN) from Urumqi, then drive 2 hours to the lake. Rent a car or join a tour.
- **Accommodation:** Stay in Yining city or lakeside guesthouses.
- **Cost:** Lake entrance fee ¥70; car entry ¥100.
- **Visa:** Xinjiang requires a valid Chinese visa; foreign travelers need to register with police.

### 4. Hebei: Mountain Escape & Summer Ski Resort (崇礼避暑)

**Route:** Beijing → Chongli (崇礼) → Yunding Ski Park (云顶滑雪公园)
**Best time:** June–August (average 20°C).

![Green mountain slopes with a cable car and resort buildings, Chongli Hebei](https://images.pexels.com/photos/12174435/pexels-photo-12174435.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&h=650&w=940)

Chongli (崇礼, Chónglǐ) in Hebei Province is a 2-hour drive from Beijing. Known for winter skiing, it transforms into a summer paradise with hiking, mountain biking, and cable car rides. The **Yunding Hotel** (云顶大酒店) offers rooms from ¥530/night.

**Must-do:** Ride the **Yunding Summit Sightseeing Cable Car** (云顶之巅观光缆车); try grass sledding or UTV off-roading.

**Practical tips:**
- **Getting there:** Drive from Beijing (2 hours) or take a high-speed train from Beijing Qinghe Station to Taizicheng Station (1.5 hours).
- **Accommodation:** Yunding Hotel or nearby guesthouses.
- **Cost:** Cable car ticket about ¥100; hotel packages from ¥806 for 2 nights.
- **Mobile payment:** Alipay/WeChat Pay accepted; bring cash for small vendors.

## Suggested Day-by-Day Itinerary (7 Days)

**Day 1–2: Dunhuang**
- Arrive, visit Mogao Caves (morning), Mingsha Mountain (afternoon).
- Overnight in Dunhuang.

**Day 3–4: Fly to Sanya**
- Beach time, visit Tianya Haijiao, snorkel at Wuzhizhou Island.
- Overnight in Sanya.

**Day 5–6: Fly to Yining, drive to Sayram Lake**
- Ring road drive, hike Songshutou, visit West Sea Grassland.
- Overnight near lake or in Yining.

**Day 7: Return to Beijing, drive to Chongli**
- Cable car ride, outdoor activities, relax at resort.
- Return to Beijing.

## Getting Around

- **Car rental:** International brands like Hertz and Avis operate in major cities. You need an International Driving Permit (IDP) and Chinese temporary license (check with rental company). Alternatively, hire a driver (¥500-800/day).
- **High-speed rail:** Connects Beijing to Chongli (1.5 hours). Book via Ctrip or 12306 app.
- **Flights:** Domestic flights are efficient; book via Alipay or Trip.com.

## Where to Stay

- **Dunhuang:** Silk Road Dunhuang Hotel (¥400-800).
- **Sanya:** Resort hotels in Yalong Bay (¥500-1500).
- **Sayram Lake:** Lakeside guesthouses (¥300-600).
- **Chongli:** Yunding Hotel (¥530-1000).

- **Sanya:** Seafood, coconut rice, Wenchang chicken.
- **Xinjiang:** Lamb pilaf (抓饭), naan bread, grilled lamb.
- **Hebei:** Local mountain vegetables, hotpot.

## Best Time to Visit

- **Dunhuang:** April–October (summer hot but manageable after 4 PM).
- **Sanya:** Year-round (summer rainy but cheaper).
- **Sayram Lake:** June–August for flowers and mild weather.
- **Chongli:** June–August for cool mountain air.

## Practical Tips

- **Visa:** Most travelers need a tourist visa (L visa). 144-hour transit visa available in select cities (Beijing, Shanghai, etc.).
- **Mobile payment:** Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay before arrival. Link your international credit card.
- **Language:** Download a translation app (e.g., Google Translate, Baidu Translate).
- **Etiquette:** Respect local customs; dress modestly in religious sites.
- **Safety:** China is generally safe; keep valuables secure.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need an International Driving Permit to drive in China?**
A: Yes, an International Driving Permit (IDP) is required, along with a temporary Chinese driving license (arranged by rental agencies). Alternatively, hire a driver.

**Q: Is it safe to drink tap water in China?**
A: No, drink bottled or boiled water. Hotels provide bottled water.

**Q: Can I use Google Maps in China?**
A: Google Maps is blocked. Use Baidu Maps or Amap (高德地图) for navigation.

**Q: What is the best way to book train tickets?**
A: Use the official 12306 app or Trip.com (English interface). You'll need your passport number.

**Q: Are credit cards widely accepted?**
A: No, mobile payment (Alipay/WeChat Pay) is king. Carry some cash for small purchases.

**Q: What should I pack for a summer road trip?**
A: Sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, light layers, comfortable walking shoes, reusable water bottle, and a power bank.

**Q: Is it easy to find halal food in China?**
A: In Xinjiang and Gansu, halal food is common. In other regions, look for清真 (qīngzhēn) signs.

**Q: Can I visit Tibet on a road trip?**
A: Tibet requires special permits and is restricted for foreign travelers. Plan with a licensed tour operator.

**Q: What is the speed limit on highways?**
A: Typically 120 km/h on expressways, 80-100 km/h on national roads, and lower in scenic areas.

**Q: Are there toll roads in China?**
A: Yes, most expressways are toll roads. Carry cash or use ETC (electronic toll collection).

**Q: What is the emergency number in China?**
A: Police: 110, Fire: 119, Ambulance: 120.
