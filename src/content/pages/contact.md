We'd love to hear from you. Whether you've spotted an error, have a travel tip to share, or want to talk about working together, there's one place to reach us.

## Email us

**[hello@roamchinatravel.com](mailto:hello@roamchinatravel.com)**

It's the fastest way to get a reply, and it reaches the whole editorial team. We aim to respond within two to three working days (Beijing time).

## What we can help with

- **Corrections & updates** — Travel rules in China change fast. If a visa rule, payment app, or transport detail in one of our guides is out of date, tell us and we'll verify and fix it, usually within a few days.
- **Tips & stories** — Found a place, a hack, or an experience other travellers should know about? Send it over. We credit contributors when we publish their tips.
- **Press & partnerships** — For sponsorships, affiliate partnerships, or content collaborations, email us with "Partnership" in the subject line.
- **Reader questions** — Planning a trip and stuck on something practical? Ask away. We can't plan full itineraries, but we'll happily point you to the right guide.

## Before you write

Many common questions are already answered in our guides:

- Visa, entry, and 240-hour transit rules
- Linking a foreign card to Alipay and WeChat Pay
- Getting online without a VPN
- Booking high-speed trains as a foreigner

Browsing the relevant guide first is often the quickest route to an answer.

## A note on what we can't do

We're an independent publication, not a travel agency. We don't book hotels, trains, or tours on your behalf, and we can't offer visa application services or one-on-one trip planning. What we *can* do is give you the clearest, most current information to plan confidently yourself.
