Jiangxi Province is one of China's least-visited regions for international tourists — which makes it one of the most rewarding. The landscapes are magnificent, the culture deep, and the crowds manageable even during peak season.

## Table of contents

## Jingdezhen (景德镇) — Porcelain Capital of the World

![— Mingcidou Old Town Scenery, Jingdezhen](/img/destinations/jiangxi-jingdezhen-ceramics-guide/1.webp)

Jingdezhen has produced porcelain for Chinese imperial courts and world trade for 1,700 years. The city's white kaolin clay and pine-wood kilns created the distinctive blue-and-white porcelain that defined world trade from the 14th century onward.

### What to Do

**Jingdezhen Ceramic Museum (景德镇陶瓷博物馆)** — Free; outstanding collection from all dynasties; full context for understanding the city.

**Taobao Street (陶宝街)** — 1km market street of ceramic shops; factory-second prices but enormous selection. Bargain on everything.

**Ceramic Art Avenue (陶艺大道)** — studios where contemporary artists work and sell; a significant percentage of China's top ceramic artists live and work in Jingdezhen.

**Sanbao International Ceramic Village (三宝国际陶艺村)** — a valley community of ceramic artists from around the world; studios open to visitors; workshops available.

**Hands-on workshops:** Numerous studios offer wheel-throwing and painting workshops. Half-day wheel workshop with bisque and glaze firing (they post it home): ¥150–¥300.

### The Jingdezhen Art Ceramics Scene

Since around 2015, Jingdezhen has developed an extraordinary contemporary ceramics scene attracting artists from across China and internationally. The area around **Sanbao** and the **Ceramic Art University (景德镇陶瓷大学)** is especially vibrant.

## Wuyuan (婺源) — China's Most Beautiful Rural Landscape

![Jinggangshan Scenery, Jiangxi](/img/destinations/jiangxi-jingdezhen-ceramics-guide/2.webp)

Wuyuan is a county of traditional Huizhou-style villages — whitewashed walls, black-tiled roofs, carved wooden interiors — set in a landscape of terraced fields, misty mountains, and flower-lined streams.

### Best Season: Late March–Early April
When the rapeseed flowers (油菜花) bloom, the entire valley turns gold — fields of yellow flowers surrounding white-walled villages, creating China's most photographed rural landscape.

### Must-Visit Villages

**Jiangwan (江湾)** — The largest and most famous Wuyuan village; well-preserved main street with ancestral halls and a Qing Dynasty government office. Entry ¥90.

**Likeng (李坑)** — A stream-side village with merchant houses reflected in the canal; the most atmospheric in late afternoon light. Entry ¥80.

**Xiaoqi (晓起)** — Quieter than the main tourist villages; extraordinary ancient camphor trees, some over 1,000 years old, shade the village lanes.

**Huangling (黄岭)** — The quintessential Wuyuan autumn photo — terraced vegetable gardens with orange persimmons hanging to dry against white walls; peak in late October.

### Getting There
From Nanchang (1.5 hours by high-speed + bus) or Shanghai (4 hours total). The most convenient way is to rent a car from the nearest town to visit multiple villages.

## Mount Lu (庐山)

![Longhu Mountain Scenery, Jiangxi](/img/destinations/jiangxi-jingdezhen-ceramics-guide/3.webp)

Mount Lu (庐山) has been a retreat for poets, politicians, and scholars for 2,000 years. The mountain's cloud-draped peaks, waterfalls, and valleys inspired countless classical poems — most famously Su Dongpo's "I cannot see the true face of Lu Mountain because I am inside it."

### Key Attractions

**Lulin Lake and Ruqin Lake** — Twin mountain lakes at 1,100m elevation; morning mist creates extraordinary atmospheric effects.

**Sandie Waterfall (三叠泉)** — "Three-Tier Falls" — 155 metres total drop through three cascades. The best-known waterfall on Mount Lu; access from the east gate of the scenic area.

**Meilu Villa Museum (庐山会议旧址)** — Mount Lu was the site of several historic Communist Party meetings; Chiang Kai-shek's summer villa and Mao Zedong's meeting rooms are preserved as museums.

### Practical Info

**Entry:** ¥160 (includes shuttle buses within the scenic area)  
**Getting there:** Bus from Nanchang (3 hours) or Jiujiang city (30 minutes)  
**Best time:** May–June (spring flowers, waterfalls full) and September–October (mist effects)  
**Altitude:** 1,474m peak; cool even in summer

## Practical Info

**Nanchang (南昌)** is Jiangxi's capital and transport hub — flights from major cities; high-speed train from Shanghai (2.5 hours), Hangzhou (2 hours). Jingdezhen has its own small airport with connections to Beijing and Shanghai.
