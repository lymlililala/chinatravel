## Why Summer 2025 Is a Great Time to Visit China

Summer 2025 brings a rare treat for travelers: a sharp drop in domestic flight fuel surcharges. Starting July 5, 2025, the fuel surcharge for flights under 800 km drops from ¥80 to ¥50, and for flights over 800 km, from ¥150 to ¥100. This means a round-trip flight from Beijing to Shanghai (over 800 km) saves you ¥100 on fuel surcharges alone. Combined with airport construction fees (typically ¥50), the total surcharge is now ¥150 for long-haul flights, down from ¥220. This change makes summer travel more affordable, especially for multi-city itineraries.

## Top Summer Deals on Flights

### How to Find Cheap Flights

Major travel platforms like Fliggy (飞猪) offer tools to snag the best prices:

- **Special Fare Search**: Search multiple origins and destinations simultaneously for up to six months ahead. Sort by price to find the cheapest travel dates.
- **Price Alert**: Set a budget for your flight, and the app will notify you when a ticket matches your target price. This 24/7 monitoring feature is perfect for flexible travelers.

For international travelers, consider using these features to book domestic flights within China. Remember to use Alipay or WeChat Pay for seamless transactions.

### Sample Routes and Prices

Here are some sample one-way fares from major cities (prices fluctuate, so always check the app):

- **Beijing to Shanghai**: from ¥400
- **Shanghai to Guangzhou**: from ¥500
- **Guangzhou to Chengdu**: from ¥600
- **Chengdu to Xi'an**: from ¥300

Prices are for economy class and exclude fuel surcharges and taxes. Booking in advance and using the price alert feature can help you lock in the best deals.

## How to Book and Save More

### Use Travel Platforms and Apps

- **Fliggy (飞猪)**: Offers special promotions for Hilton, Marriott, and other chains. For example, during the July 1-3 flash sale, Hilton Home2 Suites offers up to 50% rebate in points. Book via the app using Alipay or WeChat Pay.
- **Ctrip (携程)**: Features hotel passes and flash sales. Look for the "BOSS Price" deals highlighted in this guide.

### Leverage Loyalty Programs

- **Hilton Honors**: During the summer flash sale, earn up to 4,000 bonus points per stay. New members can get a ¥120 coupon and 3,000 bonus points after three stays.
- **Wanda Hotels**: Use your Fliggy miles for 20x value, up to 10% off room rates. Valid for properties in Quanzhou, Nanjing, Hangzhou, Dongguan, and Kunming.

### Payment & Booking Tips

- **Alipay/WeChat Pay**: Most Chinese travel platforms accept these. Set up your account before your trip.
- **Visa/Mastercard**: Accepted on some international platforms, but domestic sites may require Chinese payment methods. Consider using a travel card or linking your foreign card to Alipay.
- **Cancellation Policy**: Most hotel deals are refundable (cancel for free before a certain date). Always read the terms.

## Best Time to Visit for Deals

- **July 5 onwards**: Fuel surcharge drop takes effect, making flights cheaper.
- **Early July**: Hotel flash sales (e.g., Fliggy's July 1-3 event) offer the best prices.
- **Mid-August to September**: Post-peak season, prices for flights and hotels often drop further.
- **Avoid**: National Day Golden Week (Oct 1-7) when prices surge. If you must travel, book well in advance.

## Getting Around China in Summer

- **High-Speed Rail**: A comfortable alternative to flying. Book tickets via Ctrip or 12306.cn. Use the app in English or get help at the station.
- **Metro**: Major cities have extensive metro systems. Buy a rechargeable transit card or use Alipay's transport feature.
- **Taxis & Ride-Hailing**: Use Didi (Chinese Uber) via the app. It accepts Alipay and WeChat Pay.

## Where to Stay: Recommendations by City

- **Beijing**: For history lovers, stay near the Forbidden City. The Beijing West Railway Station Hilton Home2 Suites offers good value with easy metro access.
- **Shanghai**: Choose a hotel near the Bund or Nanjing Road. The Atour Hotel chain has several locations in the city.
- **Guangzhou**: The Guangzhou China Hotel is a classic choice in the old city, near Yuexiu Park and Shamian Island.
- **Chengdu**: Stay near Jinli Ancient Street or the Panda Base. The Wanda Reign Chengdu offers luxury with local flair.
- **Hangzhou**: For a lakeside retreat, consider the Qiandao Lake Yanshan Resort. For city convenience, the Hangzhou East Station Wanda Yihua Hotel is a great option.

## Food & Dining on a Budget

Summer in China is a feast for the senses. Don't miss:

- **Beijing**: Peking duck, zhajiangmian (noodles with bean sauce)
- **Shanghai**: Xiaolongbao (soup dumplings), shengjianbao (pan-fried buns)
- **Guangzhou**: Dim sum, roast goose
- **Chengdu**: Hotpot, mapo tofu
- **Hangzhou**: Dongpo pork, Longjing shrimp

Many hotel deals include breakfast, and some even offer dinner (like the Guangzhou China Hotel package). For budget meals, try local food streets or use delivery apps like Meituan.

## Practical Tips for Summer Travel in China

- **Weather**: Summers are hot and humid, especially in the south. Pack light clothing, sunscreen, and a hat. Rain showers are common, so carry an umbrella.
- **Hydration**: Drink plenty of water. Tap water is not potable; buy bottled water or use a purifier.
- **Etiquette**: Dress modestly when visiting temples. Learn a few Chinese phrases like "xièxiè" (thank you) and "duìbuqǐ" (sorry).
- **Money**: Carry some cash for small purchases, but most places accept mobile payments. Notify your bank before traveling to avoid card blocks.
- **Visa**: Most travelers need a tourist visa (L visa). Check if you qualify for the 144-hour transit visa-free policy in select cities like Shanghai, Beijing, and Guangzhou.
